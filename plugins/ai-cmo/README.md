# AI CMO (ChatGPT plugin)

A marketing department in one plugin. Ask in plain English and the right specialist skill picks
itself up: strategy, paid media, SEO, GEO, copywriting, earned and shared media, conversion work,
and reporting. There are no commands to learn.

This is the ChatGPT packaging of the same AI CMO that ships for Claude. Same skill, same library,
same BotBuilders Connector. Only the wrapper differs.

## What it needs

- A ChatGPT plan that allows plugins and MCP apps: Business, Enterprise or Edu with developer mode
  enabled by an admin, or Pro with developer mode on.
- A BotBuilders account with AI CMO enabled. Signing in happens once, in your browser, the first
  time the plugin reaches the library.

## Install

See `INSTALL-ChatGPT.md` in this folder.

## Good to know

- **It drafts; you approve.** It never launches an ad, spends money, publishes, sends, pitches a
  journalist, or posts on its own.
- **It will not invent a fact about your business.** No made-up metrics, testimonials, reviews,
  journalists, or follower counts. Where something is missing it names the gap.
- **If the library looks empty,** your account is not switched on for AI CMO yet. That is an account
  question: support@botbuilders.com.
